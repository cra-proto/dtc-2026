Complete part A: Applicant information

You may apply for the DTC using the digital form or the printed paper form.

Choose 1 method to apply:

If you already applied using the paper form, do not apply again using the digital form. This will delay your application.

Online

Option 1 of 3

Complete Part A of the application in your CRA account. The medical practictioner then completes Part B using an online form.

By phone

Option 2 of 3

Complete Part A of your application by phone. The medical practictioner completes Part B using an online form.

On paper

Option 3 of 3

Apply by mail using the paper form. You and the medical practictioner both complete the paper form before you mail it.

Instructions

Apply online
  1. Applicant fills out Part A

    The person with the impairment or their legal representative must fill out Part A of the digital application form:

    1. Sign in to My Account
    2. Scroll down to Benefits and credits
    3. Select Benefits and credits
    4. Select the Apply for DTC button to open the digital form

    You must answer a few questions about the person with the impairment and those who may be eligible to claim the credit on their behalf.

    Information you need to provide
    • Basic information about the person with the impairment
    • Basic information about the supporting family member who may claim the disability amount (if transferring the credit)
    • Consent for the CRA to:
      • contact the medical practitioner of the person with the impairment (if necessary)
      • automatically adjust the tax returns for previous years (if applicable)

    At the end of step 1, you will receive a reference number.

  2. Applicant gives reference number to medical practitioner

    Give the reference number to the medical practitioner. They will use it to complete and submit Part B of the digital form online. The reference number is required in order for the application form to be electronically submitted to the CRA. The reference number, last name, and date of birth must also match what was submitted in Part A.

    The number is valid until the medical practitioner submits Part B.

    You may print the reference number as a PDF. You may also view the reference number in My Account until Part B is submitted.

  3. Medical practitioner completes Part B

    Part B must only be completed by the medical practitioner. Applicants cannot complete Part B themselves. If an applicant completes Part B themselves, it will not be processed.

    Refer to Part B: Instructions for medical practitioners

    After Part B is completed, the digital form is automatically submitted to the CRA. Medical practitioners have the option to save a copy of Part B for their records.

Use the paper form

  1. Applicant fills out Part A

    The person with the impairment or their legal representative fills out Part A of the updated Form T2201, Disability Tax Credit Certificate:

     Form T2201, Disability Tax Credit Certificate

    Information you need to provide
    • Basic information about the person with the disability. All fields must be completed or your application may not be reviewed and may be returned to you
      • Name
      • Mailing address
      • Date of Birth
      • Social insurance number
    • Basic information about the supporting family member who may claim the disability amount (if transferring the credit)
    • Consent to the CRA to:
      • automatically adjust the tax returns for previous years (if applicable)
      • contact the medical practitioner of the person with the impairment (if necessary)

    After filling out Part A, print the form. The person with the disability (or their legal guardian if under 18 years old) or their legal representative must sign the authorization section of the form (Part A, Section 4) or the CRA will not process the request.

    Give the form to a medical practitioner to complete Part B.

  2. Medical practitioner completes Part B

    Part B must only be completed by the medical practitioner. Applicants cannot complete Part B themselves. If an applicant completes Part B themselves, it will not be processed.

    Refer to Complete part B: instructions for medical practitioners

    After completing Part B, the medical practitioner must sign the "Certification" section on the last page.

    Either the medical practitioner or the applicant may mail the paper form to the CRA.
