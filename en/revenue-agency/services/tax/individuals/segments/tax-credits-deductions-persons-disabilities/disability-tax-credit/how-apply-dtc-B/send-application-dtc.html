Submit your application

Either the applicant or the medical practitioner may submit the application to the CRA. If the medical practitioner is submitting it, they must mail it in.

Send us the form

Submit documents no longer supported for DTC

As of July 14, you must send your DTC application to the CRA using the digital DTC application form or by mail. You may not send DTC forms through the “submit documents” section of your CRA account. Learn more by visiting Help speed up your disability tax credit application.

If your application was completed digitally and your medical practitioner used the reference number you provided, it is automatically submitted to the CRA. If you are signed up for digital notifications from the CRA, you will receive an email informing you that your application has been successfully submitted.

If you have a PDF or scanned version of the paper form do not submit it using your CRA account. You must print and mail the paper form to the CRA.

Send paper forms by mail
